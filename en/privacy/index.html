Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your data is Dzianis Klebanovich, a private individual running Meno from Poland. For anything about personal data, write to denis.klebanovich@gmail.com. We give the postal address for correspondence on request at that e-mail; you can also find it in the developer (trader) details on Meno's App Store and Google Play pages.

What we collect

We store what you enter in the app: days and meals, repeating dishes, diets, eating style, disliked ingredients, cooking time, equipment and skill, plus your plans (with the calories and protein of each day), shopping lists, favourite recipes and ratings. Body details, activity, goal, daily calorie target and allergies are covered under "Health data". If you save your plan to an account, we store your e-mail address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, your account id with that provider; from Google we also receive your name and profile photo URL, as Google provides them. The data copy from Settings ("Download my data") includes this data too. If you sign in with Apple, we also store a token from Apple that we use only to revoke Meno's access to your Apple ID when you delete your account; it is deleted with the account. Without an account you use Meno under a random id, without giving your name or e-mail. If you turn on the reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device and is not sent from a server.

Health data

In Meno, health data means your sex, year of birth, height, weight, target weight, activity (workouts and type of work), goal and its pace, the daily calorie target, and allergies and intolerances, as well as your meal journal entries (what, when and how much you ate, with calories and macros). We store them only with your explicit consent and use them only to work out your targets, build a plan that respects them and keep your meal journal. Without consent we store none of them and the plan uses average values: 2000 kcal and protein from your eating style for a weight of 70 kg. We save the eating style (Balanced, Sporty or Keto) with your plan preferences, also without this consent. You can withdraw the consent at any time in Settings ("Withdraw the health-data consent"). We then remove your body details, activity, goal, daily calorie target and allergies from your profile, along with your whole meal journal, in one step, and later plans no longer take them into account.

Why we use your data

We use the data to work out your daily calorie and protein targets, build plans and shopping lists, keep your meal journal, save your account and move your plan between devices, run the Meno Pro subscription, send local notifications (the weekly reminder and cook-timer alerts) and, if you agree, for usage statistics. The legal basis is the contract for using the app and, for health data and statistics, your consent.

Who processes data for us

We use providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in, servers in the EU, where we keep your data), RevenueCat (subscriptions, USA, see "Purchases"), PostHog (statistics, servers in the EU, only with your consent) and Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, servers in the EU), which sends sign-in codes to your e-mail address. Apple and Google are separate controllers for their stores, payments and Apple or Google sign-in, if you choose them. Plans are built by our own program on our servers, without outside language models.

Statistics

Statistics are off until you agree. With your consent we send events to PostHog, on servers in the EU, under a random device id (this is pseudonymous data), for example which welcome screen was viewed or that a plan was built. The app adds technical device details to every event: phone model and manufacturer, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone and screen size. We never send your name, e-mail, account id, body details, activity, goal, daily targets, allergies, diets or disliked ingredients; from journal events we send only the meal type, where the entry was added from and how an entry was deleted (when targets are set we send only the eating style you picked, and about how a plan meets the targets only the number of days on, near and off target), and your IP address is not used to locate you. You can withdraw consent in Settings with the "Usage statistics" switch. After you withdraw consent or delete your account we stop sending new events, and those already sent stay in PostHog under a random id that is not linked to your account for up to 12 months.

Purchases

The App Store or Google Play handles payment for Meno Pro; we never see your card details. RevenueCat gets a pseudonymous Meno account id, the purchase details and the technical device data needed to process the purchase, including your IP address, and we switch Meno Pro on based on them. RevenueCat processes this data in the USA; we transfer it under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while you have an account. An account with no saved e-mail or Apple or Google sign-in that nobody has used for 90 days is deleted automatically with its data, unless it has active Meno Pro or Meno Pro that ended in the last 30 days. Journal entries older than 12 months are deleted automatically. Purchase event records, which contain no personal data, are deleted after 180 days. PostHog keeps statistics events for up to 12 months, and the hosting provider keeps technical sign-in and server function logs for up to 7 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all data in Settings ("Delete account and data"), which you reach with the profile button at the top of the screen and then "Settings": your profile, plans, journal, shopping lists, favourites and ratings are removed at once. Only these remain: the sign-in token on your device, which expires on its own shortly, technical sign-in and server function logs that the provider keeps for up to 7 days, and pseudonymous statistics events already sent to PostHog, not linked to your account, for up to 12 months. Apple and Google keep the purchase history under their own rules (they are separate controllers), and we delete your subscriber record at RevenueCat together with the account. Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play settings. Meno Pro paid for in the store comes back on a new account with "Restore purchases".

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can download a copy of your data in Settings ("Download my data"), and use your other rights by writing to denis.klebanovich@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, PUODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.
