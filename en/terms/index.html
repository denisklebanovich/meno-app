Health

Meno is not medical or dietary advice. Calculated calories are an estimate from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, and protein follows the eating style you pick and your weight; calories you change yourself are used as you set them. The plan meets your daily targets approximately and always shows the actual numbers. Your plan leaves out the allergens you enter, but always check the ingredients on the product label. If you have allergies, a medical condition or an eating disorder, or are pregnant, talk to a doctor or dietitian about your diet.
