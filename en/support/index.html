Support

Common questions about the Meno app. Can't find an answer? Write to us, we reply within 14 days.

How do I cancel my Meno Pro subscription?

Manage the subscription in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting your Meno account does not cancel it.

How do I restore purchases?

In the app, tap the profile button at the top, then "Settings" and "Meno Pro", and tap "Restore purchases". Use the store account Meno Pro was bought with.

How do I download my data?

In the app, tap the profile button at the top, then "Settings", and under "Privacy" choose "Download my data".

How do I withdraw my health-data consent?

In the app, tap the profile button at the top, then "Settings", and under "Account and data" choose "Withdraw the health-data consent". We then delete your body details, activity, goal, daily calorie target, allergies and your whole meal journal.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap the profile button at the top, then "Settings", and under "Account and data" choose "Delete account and data". See the account deletion page for details.
