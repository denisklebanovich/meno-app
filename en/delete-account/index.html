Delete your Meno account

The Meno app, developer: Dzianis Klebanovich. Here is how to delete your account and data.

Deleting in the app

  1. Open Meno and tap the profile button at the top of the screen.
  2. Choose "Settings".
  3. Under "Account and data", choose "Delete account and data".
  4. Confirm with "Delete for good".

What we delete immediately

  • profile
  • plans
  • meal journal
  • shopping lists
  • favourites
  • recipe ratings

What remains and for how long

  • The sign-in token on your device: it expires on its own after a short time.
  • Technical server logs: up to 7 days.
  • Pseudonymous statistics events already sent to PostHog, not linked to your account: up to 12 months.
  • Apple and Google keep the purchase history under their own rules.
  • We delete your subscriber record at RevenueCat together with the account.

Subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Can't use the app?

Write to denis.klebanovich@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your Meno account (or the e-mail you use with Apple or Google sign-in) with the subject "Meno account deletion". We delete the account within 14 days.

Accounts without an e-mail

An anonymous account, with no e-mail address, can be deleted only in the app. We also delete it automatically after 90 days of inactivity, unless it has active Meno Pro or Meno Pro that ended in the last 30 days.
